Method: 作者提出了一个系统性的框架，用于分类可能的FQH态并在量子电路上制备它们。该框架的核心发现是：团簇化的非阿贝尔FQH态（如parafermionic Read-Rezayi态）可以通过并行的量子制备电路实现，双量子比特门深度与系统尺寸无关（恒为3层）；而阿贝尔Laughlin态则需要门深度随系统尺寸线性增长的顺序电路链。制备完成后，通过测量恢复分数准空穴电荷，并通过干涉测量扩展获取非阿贝尔e/4准空穴的编织数据。

Result: 成功制备了从8到118量子比特的Read-Rezayi Z₃态（深度恒为3层），以及154量子比特、104电子的Read-Rezayi Z₄态，并实现了全根采样。共展示了18族FQH态，覆盖IBM Heron处理器的全部156量子比特。测量恢复了预期的分数准空穴电荷，对团簇态而言电荷估计器在每个对称性选择的shot中精确成立；同时通过干涉测量方法获取了非阿贝尔e/4准空穴的编织数据。

Conclusion: 该工作建立了在量子处理器上可扩展地研究FQH物理的路线，为制备和探测远远超出传统平台能力的非阿贝尔拓扑物质开辟了新途径。

Abstract: Non-Abelian anyons arise as exotic excitations in fractional quantum Hall (FQH) matter and have proved very elusive to realize in conventional platforms. In this work, we show that on a programmable quantum hardware platform, the more exotic FQH excitations are the less costly ones to prepare: clustered non-Abelian FQH states admit parallel quantum preparation circuits whose two-qubit depth is independent of system size, while constructing the more common Abelian Laughlin state requires a sequential circuit chain with linear depth. The centerpiece of this work is our new systematic framework for cataloging possible FQH states and preparing them on quantum circuits at unprecedented scale and variety. Our prepared parafermionic Read--Rezayi $\mathbb{Z}_3$ state holds depth 3 from 8 to 118 qubits, and full root sampling extends to a 154-qubit, 104-electron Read--Rezayi $\mathbb{Z}_4$ state. In all, our demonstrated 18-family catalog of prepared FQH states extends to all 156 qubits of an IBM Heron processor, limited only by existing hardware scale. Measurements on the prepared states recover the expected fractional quasihole charges, with the charge estimator exact in every symmetry-selected shot for the clustered states, and braiding data of the non-Abelian $e/4$ quasihole measured via interferometric extensions. Our work establishes a scalable route to studying FQH physics on quantum processors and opens new avenues for preparing and probing non-Abelian topological matter far beyond the reach of conventional platforms.
