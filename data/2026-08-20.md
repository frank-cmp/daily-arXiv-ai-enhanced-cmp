Result: LRBM模型在上述三种数据类型上均呈现层级逻辑：低层编码全局结构，高层编码细粒度特征。在严格实验中，作者构建并评估了160条合成与1130条天然分支酸变位酶（CM）的构造轨迹，所设计的CM酶与最近天然同源物序列差异最高达53%。酶折叠在LRBM中间层已出现，但功能仅在满足所有层级后才产生，表明折叠对该酶家族是功能必要但非充分条件。

Conclusion: 结果表明，构造涌现系统存在一种共享的、分层且可学习的统计逻辑；LRBM提供了一种显式约束信息流以逐步构建涌现系统的新途径，并阐明了折叠与功能之间的层级关系。

Abstract: Emergent systems - systems containing multi-scale interactions - often arise through iteration rather than explicit forward design. As such, design principles for building emergent systems have been under-explored. Current artificial intelligence architectures, while useful for generation, do not provide a logic for how to construct emergent systems. Inspired by statistical phylogenetics, we show that inferring scales of system entropy then explicitly constraining information flow from low to high entropic scales yields data-driven, stepwise 'constructive models' of emergent systems. We term the resulting architecture the Layer-Restricted Boltzmann Machine (LRBM). Applied identically to digit images, natural language, and enzyme sequences, LRBM models follow hierarchical logic: lower layers encode global structure from which higher layers encode fine-grained features. As a stringent experimental challenge for LRBM-based construction, we built and evaluated 160 constructive trajectories of synthetic and 1,130 natural chorismate mutase (CM) enzymes in an in vivo functional assay. Designed CMs functioned up to 53% sequence divergent from the nearest natural homolog. Notably, enzyme fold emerged at intermediate LRBM layers while function arose only upon satisfying all layers. Fold was therefore necessary but insufficient for function in this enzyme family. Our results suggest a shared hierarchical, learnable statistical logic for constructing emergent systems.
