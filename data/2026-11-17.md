Abstract: We study the impact of quenched extended defects correlated in $ε_d$ dimensions and randomly distributed in the remaining $d - ε_d$ dimensions on the critical properties of a $d$-dimensional $O(m)$-symmetric system with cubic anisotropy. Exploiting the field-theoretic renormalization-group approach within the minimal subtraction scheme, we compute the two-loop renormalization-group functions. Applying resummation techniques to analyze the asymptotic series, we estimate the marginal values of $ε_d$ above which disorder becomes relevant for the physically important cases $m = 2$ and $m = 3$ in $d = 3$.
