Motivation: 传统观点认为耗散会使系统偏离临界点并抑制大事件，而OFC模型在非保守区却表现出非普适的雪崩指数（τ>3/2）且大事件持续存在。作者希望验证这些"异常"特征是否能在真实实验系统中复现，从而判断OFC类动力学是否具有普遍相关性。

Method: 在二维颗粒系统中进行实验，通过增大颗粒间摩擦以增强耗散，观测雪崩事件统计；测量雪崩尺寸指数 τ 随耗散的变化、上限截断与系统尺寸的关系；进一步对比实验与OFC模型在记忆效应、局部动力学以及大事件可预测性（优于随机预测）上的相似性。

Result: 增大颗粒间摩擦（即增大耗散）后，雪崩尺寸指数从 τ=1.58 上升至 τ=1.83，同时上限截断仍与系统尺寸成正比，系统尺度的大事件持续存在。实验还表现出与OFC模型相似的记忆效应和局部动力学，且大事件具有优于随机的可预测性，表明系统并非始终处于临界态，而是在不同"极端事件倾向性"的构型间演化。

Conclusion: OFC类动力学并非某一模型的异常特性，而可作为理解真实耗散系统中 τ>3/2 标度不变动力学的有效框架；系统并非永久临界，而是通过具有不同极端事件倾向性的构型演化。

Abstract: Scale-invariant avalanche dynamics are commonly associated with criticality and robust, universal size exponents. Dissipation is generally expected to drive the system away from the critical point, progressively suppressing large events. The Olami--Feder--Christensen (OFC) model challenges this picture: in its non-conservative regime, the avalanche-size exponent is non-universal and can exceed the mean-field value $τ=3/2$, while system-spanning events persist even at large dissipation. Here, we show that these apparently anomalous features are also observed experimentally in a two-dimensional granular system displaying scale-invariant avalanche dynamics. By increasing interparticle friction, and therefore dissipation, the avalanche-size exponent increases from $τ=1.58$ to $τ=1.83$, while the upper cutoff remains proportional to the system size. We further identify similarities between experiment and the OFC model in their memory effects, local dynamics, and the emergence of better-than-random predictability of large events. The latter indicates that the system does not remain permanently critical, but instead evolves through configurations with different propensities to generate extreme events. Our results suggest that OFC-like dynamics are not merely an anomalous feature of a particular model, but may provide a relevant framework for understanding scale-invariant dynamics with $τ>3/2$ in real dissipative systems.
