TL;DR: 本文研究了源自对具有离散全局对称群 G 的理论进行 H-gauging 后产生的不可逆选择定则，建立了包含广义场变换的 H-gauged 模型一般框架，并通过半直积 G ⋊ H 的投影特征标给出 n 点裸耦合非零的充要条件。

Motivation: 在非阿贝尔群情形下，H 诱导的变换会非平凡地混合 G 多重态的内部分量，并可能投影掉某些自由度，导致基于标准张量积分解或共轭类的常规选择定则失效，因此需要发展能系统描述这类 H-gauged 理论及其选择定则的新框架。

Method: 作者提出一个包含广义场变换的 H-gauged 模型一般框架，利用半直积 G ⋊ H 分析整体对称性结构；引入投影特征标（projected characters）来刻画保留下来的场分量，并据此推导 n 点裸耦合非零的充分必要条件；同时利用 Clebsch-Gordan 系数证明剩余场分量满足一个结合的类融合代数。

Result: 得到以下主要结果：(1) 建立了可处理非阿贝尔群（多维不可约表示起关键作用）的 H-gauged 模型框架；(2) 用投影特征标给出了 n 点裸耦合非零的充要条件；(3) 证明剩余场分量按 Clebsch-Gordan 系数构成结合（融合式）代数；(4) 表明这些选择定则限制了允许的相互作用，并在耦合常数之间施加特定关系；(5) 通过 Δ(54) ≅ Δ(27) ⋊ Z₂ 和 S₄ ≅ A₄ ⋊ Z₂ 等具体例子加以验证。

Conclusion: 不可逆选择定则可通过半直积 G ⋊ H 的投影特征标系统刻画，剩余场分量遵循由 Clebsch-Gordan 系数支配的融合型代数；这些规则在唯象上约束了允许的相互作用和耦合常数关系，所提出的框架为理解非阿贝尔离散对称群的 H-gauged 理论提供了统一工具。

Abstract: We investigate non-invertible selection rules originating from the discrete $H$-gauging of theories with an underlying discrete global symmetry group $G$. To systematically describe these theories, we formulate a general framework for $H$-gauged models that incorporates generalized field transformations. Our approach naturally accommodates non-Abelian groups, for which multidimensional irreducible representations play an essential role. In such models with non-Abelian groups, the transformations induced by $H$ non-trivially mix the internal components of $G$-multiplets, potentially projecting out specific degrees of freedom. Consequently, conventional selection rules based on standard tensor product decompositions or conjugacy classes become insufficient. By analyzing the full semidirect product $G \rtimes H$, we introduce projected characters to derive necessary and sufficient conditions for non-vanishing $n$-point bare couplings. Furthermore, we demonstrate that the remaining field components obey an associative fusion-like algebra governed by their Clebsch-Gordan coefficients. Phenomenologically, these selection rules restrict allowed interactions and impose specific relations among coupling constants. We illustrate our results through concrete examples, including $Δ(54) \cong Δ(27)\rtimes \mathbb{Z}_2$ and $S_4 \cong A_4 \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_2$.
